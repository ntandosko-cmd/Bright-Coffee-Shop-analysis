# Bright Coffee Shop Analysis

## Overview

This repository contains an analysis of Bright Coffee Shop’s performance across multiple locations, products, and time periods. The goal is to identify key revenue drivers and provide actionable recommendations for future growth.

## Mission

Bright Coffee Shop aims to be a recognised leader in premium bagged coffees and specialty coffee beverages.

## Contents

- **Presentation PDF:** Full analysis and visual summary
- **Data:** Revenue breakdowns by product, location, day, and hour

## Data & Tools Used

- **Data Platform:**  
  SQL (Snowflake) was used for data extraction, transformation, and analysis.
- **Data Visualisation:**  
  Microsoft Excel was used to create charts and visual summaries.
- **Presentation:**  
  Microsoft PowerPoint was used to compile and present the findings.

## Key Insights

- **Product Revenue:**  
  Coffee and tea are the largest contributors, accounting for 39% and 28% of total revenue, respectively.
- **Top-Selling Products:**  
  Barista Espresso, Brewed Chai tea, and Hot Chocolate are among the top 10 best sellers.
- **Location Performance:**  
  All three locations (Astoria, Hell’s Kitchen, Lower Manhattan) more than doubled their revenue over the past 6 months. Hell’s Kitchen showed the highest percentage growth (approx. 134%).
- **Time-Based Trends:**  
  Revenue peaks between 8–11am, with 10am being the highest hour for all locations. Weekdays account for 72% of total revenue.

## Recommendations

- **Focus on Peak Hours:**  
  Allocate more staff and resources during late morning (especially around 10am).
- **Leverage Top-Selling Products:**  
  Promote coffee and tea, and highlight best sellers in marketing and displays.
- **Optimise for Weekday Sales:**  
  Tailor operations and promotions to maximise weekday performance.
- **Boost Evening Sales:**  
  Develop new offers or events to increase revenue after 4pm.
